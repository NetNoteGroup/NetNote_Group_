About NetNote Aero

NetNote Aero brings aerial technology to work. From mapping land and inspecting roofs to filming projects and training new pilots, we turn the view from above into information you can act on.

NetNote Aero is part of NetNote Group, so you can also draw on the skills of our sister companies when a project needs them. Behind it is more than seven years of experience.

Why choose us

  • Faster than ground-based surveys
  • Dated, repeatable records you can compare over time
  • Safety-first flight planning that follows local aviation rules
  • Deliverables in formats your team can use
