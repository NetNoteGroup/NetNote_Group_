Aerial Survey & Mapping

Accurate maps and 3D models from the air.

Request this serviceAbout NetNote Aero

Overview

We capture overlapping aerial images and process them into maps, models and measurements for land, construction and planning projects.

Delivered by NetNote Aero, part of NetNote Group, with more than seven years of experience behind it.

What’s included

  • Orthomosaic maps and 3D models
  • Site progress monitoring
  • Area, distance and volume measurements
  • Repeat surveys to track change
  • Delivery in common CAD and GIS formats

Why it matters

See the whole site

Capture large or hard-to-reach areas in a fraction of the time.

Safer work

Inspect roofs, towers and structures without sending people up.

Records you can compare

Dated imagery lets you track progress and change over time.

Starting rates

Starting prices in Kenya shillings. Your final quote depends on the site and scope.

Frequently asked questions

How accurate is it?

Accuracy depends on equipment and ground control. We agree the accuracy you need before the flight.

How long does a survey take?

Flights are often completed in a day; processing follows.
