Frequently asked questions

Quick answers about working with NetNote Group.

Where are you based?

Our office is in Kahawa Sukari, Nairobi, Kenya. We serve clients across Kenya and can support many services remotely.

How much experience do you have?

We bring more than seven years of hands-on experience in IT, security and aerial technology.

How do I get a quote?

Use the quote form on the website, send a WhatsApp message or call us. We will reply with options and a price.

Do you offer monthly plans?

Yes. Many services, especially IT support and security, are available on a monthly plan.

Do you support our existing equipment?

Yes. We audit what you have and recommend upgrades only where they make sense.

Can you build custom software?

Yes. We build custom tools and also configure ERP and off-the-shelf systems.

Can you fix our Wi-Fi?

Usually. A site survey shows whether the problem is placement, interference or equipment.

Are your drone flights legal?

We plan every flight to follow the aviation rules that apply and agree permissions before flying.

What do I receive after a survey?

Maps, 3D models, measurements and reports in formats your team can use.

Can I learn to fly drones with you?

Yes. We run beginner and advanced training for individuals and teams.

Is my small business at risk?

Yes. Small businesses are common targets because they are often less protected. A security assessment shows where to start.

Do you test backups?

Yes. A backup is only proven once it has been restored, so we test regularly.

Can I view cameras from my phone?

Yes, securely, from your phone or computer.
