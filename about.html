About NetNote Group

A Nairobi-based group of three companies built on seven years of practical technology experience.

Who we are

NetNote Group is a technology group based in Nairobi, Kenya. We began with IT support, networks and web work, and as clients asked for more, we grew into three focused companies: NetNote Systems for IT and software, NetNote Aero for drone and aerial services, and NetNote Security for cyber and physical protection.

With more than seven years of experience, we know that technology is only useful when it is reliable, secure and understood by the people who use it. That is how we design, deliver and support everything we do.
