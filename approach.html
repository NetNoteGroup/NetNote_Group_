How we work

A simple, transparent process that keeps projects on track and clients informed.

Our five-step process

Select a step to see what happens and what you can expect.

Discover

We listen first. We learn how your business works, what is not working and what success looks like. We visit your site or run a short call, and we audit what you already have.

Design

We propose a clear scope, options and a fixed price or monthly plan. You see the plan, the timeline and the risks before anything starts.

Deliver

We build, install or fly the project, test it thoroughly and keep you updated at every milestone, with no surprises.

Document

You receive clear documentation and training, so your team understands the system and you are never dependent on us to use it.

Support

After handover we stay available for support, maintenance and improvements, on call or on a monthly plan.

Ways to work with us

Project

A defined piece of work with a clear scope, timeline and price. Best for websites, installations, surveys and migrations.

Monthly plan

Ongoing support, monitoring and maintenance for a predictable monthly cost. Best for IT support and security.

On demand

Call us when you need us, for troubleshooting, one-off jobs and urgent help.
